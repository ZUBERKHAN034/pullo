# Code of Conduct

Pullo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults, discrimination and publishing other people's private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer, [@jordan-cod](https://github.com/jordan-cod), privately, through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabrieljordandev/). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.
